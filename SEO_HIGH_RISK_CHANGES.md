# High-Risk Changes Register

Every change in this implementation that can move rankings, traffic, Ads quality or revenue, with
the guard that was applied and the rollback. «Risk» is the worst plausible effect, not the
expected one. Nothing here changed a URL, a slug, an H1, a form, a tracking tag or a city page
other than Gainesville and Ocala.

## Not done on purpose (would have been high risk)

- No slug, URL, redirect-to-consolidate or 301 for any blog or city page (Tier 2 declined in
  `docs/encargos/SEO-AEO-GEO-PLAN.md` §6; Ads Final URLs active).
- No `aggregateRating`, postal address, `geo`, opening hours in schema (unverified).
- No change to forms, `src/pages/api/formulario.ts`, GTM/GA4/Ads tags, `/thank-you`.
- No edit to Webflow body copy frozen by `check:texto` on the hubs, regional hubs or 51 cities.
- No «Florida Project Showcase» heading relabel on city pages (carousel heading is anchored by
  the frozen baseline; relabel needs a declared text line, logged in `REQUIRES_CLIENT_CONFIRMATION`).
- No Studio deploy, no production deploy, no merge: the branch stops at `git push`.
