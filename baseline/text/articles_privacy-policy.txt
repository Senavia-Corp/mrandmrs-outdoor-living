Privacy Policy
Mr & Mrs Outdoor Living values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information when you:
Fill out a contact or estimate form
Schedule a consultation
Contact us via email or phone
This information may include your name, email address, phone number, project details, and location.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
Respond to inquiries and requests
Provide estimates and consultations
Improve our services and website experience
Communicate about your project or services requested
Information Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except when required to provide services or comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights
