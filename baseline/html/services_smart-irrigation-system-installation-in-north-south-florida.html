Smart Irrigation FAQs

Have questions about smart irrigation system installation in North or South Florida? Our certified Irrigreen specialists answer the top questions here.

How do smart irrigation controllers work in Florida?

Smart controllers use evapotranspiration sensors and local weather data to automatically adjust irrigation schedules for precise, efficient watering in Florida landscapes. As a certified Irrigreen dealer, Mr. & Mrs. Outdoor Living installs systems that respond to real-time conditions — reducing overwatering and delivering measurable water savings.

What are the benefits of drip irrigation systems?

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to plant roots, dramatically reducing evaporation, weed growth, and fungal disease in Florida landscapes. This targeted approach uses significantly less water than traditional sprinkler systems — making it ideal for South Florida homeowners navigating local water restrictions and rising utility costs.

Can smart irrigation reduce my water bill in Florida?

Yes. Smart irrigation systems with evapotranspiration sensors prevent overwatering by adjusting schedules based on real-time weather and soil conditions. Florida homeowners who upgrade from traditional timer-based systems consistently report significant monthly water savings, with many reducing irrigation usage by 30% or more.

Can irrigation zones be customized for my property?

Yes. Smart irrigation systems include individual zones designed around your property's specific plant types, soil conditions, and sun exposure. Zones can be adjusted seasonally as your landscape evolves. Our certified irrigation contractors program and test every zone before handover to ensure optimal performance across your Florida property.

Do you offer maintenance services after installation?

We offer seasonal maintenance, system repairs, controller programming updates, and technology upgrades to keep your smart irrigation system running at peak efficiency across North and South Florida. Our certified team responds quickly to service requests, ensuring your landscape stays healthy without wasted water or unexpected failures.
