Our Comprehensive Outdoor Living Process

We deliver each project with precision, transparency, and minimal disruption to your operations.

1
2
3
4

1. Design & Planning

We begin with a personalized consultation and site evaluation to understand your vision and outdoor space. Our team develops a complete 3D design to visualize the project and ensure every element works together seamlessly before construction begins.

2. Permits & Site Preparation

We handle all engineering, permits, and approvals required in Florida. Once approved, we prepare the site with proper excavation, structural framing, and foundational work to ensure long-term durability and safety.

3. Construction & Finishes

Our team completes all structural and installation work with precision, followed by high-quality finishes and materials. From integrated features to equipment, electrical, and safety requirements, every detail is built to meet the highest standards.

4. Final Inspection & Handover

We complete the project with a detailed inspection and walkthrough, ensuring everything meets our quality, safety, and craftsmanship standards. Once approved, your outdoor space is ready to enjoy with confidence and peace of mind.
