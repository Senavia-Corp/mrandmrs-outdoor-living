Daily and Weekly Maintenance Schedules

Start with daily chemical balancing by checking pH between 7.2 and 7.8, and chlorine at 1-3 ppm. Skim surface debris before it sinks, especially after heavy use in commercial settings. This prevents cloudy water and swimmer irritation.

Weekly, clean filters by backwashing and inspect for clogs. Vacuum the pool floor and brush vertical surfaces to remove buildup. Use a checklist to ensure consistency across staff shifts.

  • Test and adjust water chemistry every morning.
  • Remove leaves and trash from skimmers.
  • Run the pump for at least 8-12 hours daily.
  • Shock the pool if bather load increases.

These steps keep water safe and inviting, minimizing downtime for high-traffic commercial pools.
