Cleaning Routines for Pool Finishes and Surfaces

Regular cleaning routines protect pool finishes on new residential pool construction. Skim surface debris daily and vacuum weekly using soft brushes. Avoid abrasive tools on fiberglass pools or vinyl pools to prevent scratches.

In South Florida pools like Naples pools, rinse tiles monthly to combat mineral buildup. Empty skimmer baskets and clean ladders. These steps preserve the look from the pool design process.
